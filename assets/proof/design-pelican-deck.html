Section 01

The Mount Problem

  • a.Anatomy check: wings built for soaring, not handlebars — but a 3 m wingspan doubles as excellent built-in stabilization at speed.
  • b.Geometry check: standard frame sizing assumes legs, not legs plus a webbed semi-paddle; seat height must accommodate a generous undercarriage pouch.
  • c.The compromise: a step-through frame with dropped bars keeps the beak clear of the stem — mounting achieved in under four flap-assisted seconds.

Section 02 · Findings

Verdict: Feasible

  • 1.Balance: solved — birds already manage on one leg; two wheels plus a pelican is statistically a luxury.
  • 2.Propulsion: webbed feet, meet clipless pedals. Nature has been running the beta test for millennia.
  • 3.Cargo capacity: the pouch remains the industry's only saddle bag that doubles as carry-on luggage.

Recommendation: fund the pilot program immediately. The pelicans are ready.
